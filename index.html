03 · About

The project

The Artful Dodger is the dark drum & bass / techno project of Seth, operating out of Bennington, Vermont.

Seventeen years after the debut album, the project is back in the lab — new singles, new machines, same late-night frequencies. Music made the hard way: written, produced, and programmed by hand (and sometimes in Python).
